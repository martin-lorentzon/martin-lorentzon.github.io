B. Clay & tape

Gothenburg and the car industry

The site is a car-design studio's clay model: content arrives as matte grey clay, and the tape lines designers lay on clay draw the layout. The finished render shows only inside shapes the tape has closed.

Produktfilm, interaktiv 3D och verktyg från en 3D-generalist med bakgrund i bilindustrin. Göteborg.

Mona Sans, one variable family: wide and heavy for headlines, normal width for text. Martian Mono for dimension call-outs. Google Fonts.

C. Node graph

How the work gets done

The site is a node graph. The visitor's idea is the input node, each service is a node with real outputs (film, web 3D, a tool), and wires connect them as you scroll down to the contact node. The page is the pipeline.

Canvas#15181D

Ink#E9ECEF
Muted#8A93A0
Geometry#00D6A3

Type

Supreme for headlines and text (Fontshare), Martian Mono for node labels and socket names (Google Fonts).

Motion & 3D

Signature: wires draw from socket to socket as you scroll and a pulse runs along them. Hover a socket to light up its whole path. Wire colours follow Blender's socket colours, so each service has a colour that means something. The popcorn stays: the kernel in "Din idé" pops when its first wire connects. Motion verb: connect, snap to the socket and flow. SVG wires plus the existing Three.js hero.

References

spline.designrive.appcables.gl

Risk

Can feel technical to product-film buyers. Fix: plain Swedish copy on every node, and the graph is a reading aid, never required to navigate.

1. Skiss → Klart

Sketch to finished. Round 1's A, in plain words

One object travels down the page and is finished in four steps anyone recognises: sketch, shape, light, done. Each case study runs the same four steps with the client's own object, so a product, a heart valve or a tool all tell the same story.

till klart.

Palette

Studio light#FFC44D

Type

Switzer for headlines and text, Zodiak italic only for the four step names (Skiss, Form, Ljus, Klart). Both from Fontshare.

Motion & 3D

Signature: as you scroll, one 3D object is drawn as pencil lines, filled in as grey shape, lit, then shown finished in colour, with a progress bar that fills step by step. The colour arrives only at "Klart". Motion verb: develop, like a photo coming up in the tray. Three.js, which the site already uses. With reduced motion, the four steps sit side by side as stills.

References

apple.com/airpods-prolusion.coigloo.inc

Risk

Close to round 1's A, so it is the safe pick. It needs one good hero object, and each case study needs a few in-between renders (sketch, grey, lit) as well as the final.

2. Gör det synligt

Make it visible

The company's real job is showing what's hard to see: a product before the prototype, the inside of a body, a workflow nobody can picture. The site has a lens: point at anything and it shows what's inside or behind it.

Det du inte ser,
gör vi synligt.

3D-film, interaktiv 3D och verktyg som förklarar produkter, kroppen och processer.

ProdukterMedicinUtbildningIndustriVerktyg
Inuti: motor, lager, kretskort

Palette

Ground#FFFFFF
Ink#111111
Muted#6B6B6B
Line#E4E4E4
Inside#3A3AF0
Lens#F4F4FF

Type

General Sans for everything, friendly and precise. One colour, ultramarine, is kept for things you can only see through the lens. Fontshare.

Motion & 3D

Signature: a round lens follows the pointer over the hero and the case images and shows the inside view: a cutaway, the organ under the skin, the steps behind a button. On phones it follows the scroll. Motion verb: reveal, a soft circle that opens. Mostly two stacked images and a CSS mask; Three.js only in the hero.

References

ciechanow.skiapple.com/iphonelusion.co

Risk

Every showcase needs a matching outside and inside image. A great fit for products and medicine; it takes more thought for tools and add-ons (the "inside" is the workflow they replace).

3. Låt den poppa

Let it pop. Your own line, taken all the way

"Låt din idé poppa" becomes the whole site. Every idea starts as a small, hard kernel (a brief, a sketch, a CAD file) and pops into the finished result when you reach it. Everyone knows popcorn, so it works in any field.

Låt din idé poppa.
Produktfilm, 45 s, klar för lansering
Före: en skiss och tre CAD-filer

Palette

Ground#1A1714
Ink#FFFAF0
Muted#B7AB9B
Kernel#C8741E
Butter#FFD54A
Pop#FF5A1F

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, which have a bit of bounce, and Host Grotesk for text. Google Fonts.

Motion & 3D

Signature: the existing popcorn hero, and every case study is a "kernel" card (the brief, in the client's words) that pops open into the finished film or viewer when you reach it. Motion verb: pop, a short squash then a springy burst. Reuses the Three.js popcorn you already have.

References

cuberto.comlocomotive.calusion.co

Risk

Playful by nature. Fine for products and launches, but it may feel light for medical or industrial buyers. Keep the case-study pages calm and let the pop happen once per project.
