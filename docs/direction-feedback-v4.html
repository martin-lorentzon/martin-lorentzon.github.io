One full turn. The whole page is a turntable

One product stands on a turntable for the whole visit. Scrolling turns it, and the page is exactly one revolution: 0° is the welcome, 90° film, 180° interactive 3D, 270° tools, 360° contact. The sentence. The brief is one line you finish

The front page is a sentence addressed to Martin with blanks to fill: "Hi Martin, I have a new product and need a film for the launch in March." Every blank is a menu. As you change it, the example beside it changes to real work that fits, and Martin's usual answer appears underneath. Press send and the sentence arrives as a text or an e-mail. A conversation, not a sales page.
