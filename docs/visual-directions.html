Company site: three visual directions

Phase 1 of the refresh of martin-lorentzon.github.io for Lorentzon Visualisation AB. An audit of today's site, then three directions to choose from. Nothing is built yet. Mock copy is in Swedish, like the site.

1. Audit of the live site (24 Nov 2026)

What works

  • The popcorn hero is real Three.js with physics, built from the founder's own model. It is the one memorable thing on the site.
  • Light page: about 191 KB in total, no console errors, no layout shift (CLS 0).
  • The pinned full-screen views pattern in Infografik is solid and documented.
  • Reduced-motion and phone-specific settings already exist in the hero code.

What holds it back

  • No work on the site. No projects, films or add-ons; "Tidigare arbeten" links out to an old Notion page. The Blender add-ons the company sells aren't mentioned.
  • Placeholder images (placehold.co "1920 × 1080") in Infografik and Om mig.
  • Phone load: on a throttled mid-range phone the headline appeared after 21.7 s (desktop 2.9 s), and the popcorn hadn't shown up in the phone screenshot.
  • Looks like a default template: stock daisyUI look, centred headline with one gradient word, a lilac default button. Nothing in the type, colour or layout says "3D studio".
  • Accessibility: 5 form inputs without labels (menu toggle, FAQ), no skip link, focus lands on hidden inputs, 7 footer/contact links under the 24 px tap-target size, "Lanseringserbjudande" is a link with nowhere to go.
  • Sharing & search: no social preview image or Open Graph tags, no favicon, canonical link, robots.txt or sitemap. Company name and org nr aren't on the page.

2. Three directions

Each takes its idea from a different place: the craft (A), the city and the car industry (B), how the work actually gets done (C). Scores are 1–5 for originality, fit, how buildable it is, and how easily it could be moved to someone else (lower is better).

B. Clay & tape

Gothenburg and the car industry

The site is a car-design studio's clay model: content arrives as matte grey clay, and the tape lines designers lay on clay draw the layout. The finished render shows only inside shapes the tape has closed.

C. Node graph

How the work gets done Recommended

The site is a node graph. The visitor's idea is the input node, each service is a node with real outputs (film, web 3D, a tool), and wires connect them as you scroll down to the contact node. The page is the pipeline.

3. Recommendation

Go with C, Node graph. It is the only direction that ties all three services and the Blender add-ons together in one system, and it reads as a sales path: your idea in, a finished result out, then contact. It keeps the popcorn and the "poppa" line, and it gives a natural place for case studies (each project is a small graph from input to output).

From B, take the dimension call-outs for project captions. From A, take render noise that clears for the loader.

Whichever you pick, the rebuild should also fix the audit items: real work on the site, the phone load time, labels and tap targets, and sharing tags.
