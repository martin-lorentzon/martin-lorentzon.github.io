X. Signerat

Signed. Finished work, with Martin's own notes on it

Every piece of work is shown big and finished, like a gallery print. Then the work is "signed": Martin's own hand-written notes sit on top of it and explain one decision each. Move the pointer over the image and part of it turns back into the wire model underneath. The work sells; the notes make it personal.

Y. Namnet

The name. A bold wordmark the work lives inside

"LORENTZON" fills the screen in heavy wide type, built in 3D. As the page scrolls, a cut line sweeps across the letters: behind it they're wire, in front they're finished, polished renders. Then the letters open and become frames for the work. Bold and expensive, and nobody else can have it, because it's the name.

Z. Göteborgsljus

Gothenburg light. The work lit by the real sky outside

Lighting is the core skill behind every render, and Martin works in Gothenburg, under a low Nordic sun. The hero shows a finished render lit by the sun's real position over Gothenburg right now: long shadows in winter, blue dusk in the afternoon, a night set after dark. Visitors see the work, and where and when it's made.
